# butterfly-effect
A small hand gesture changes the whole mood of a picture. Made by Nazik Iliazova for Soul Camp, tech track, in two days as a beginner, with Claude.

Live demo: https://cheesesaucee.github.io/butterfly-effect/
How to use: open it in Chrome and click “Turn on hand control”. A fist folds the portrait, an open hand unfolds it, moving your hand turns it, and a pinch changes the mood. The butterflies follow your index finger. Your camera never leaves your device.
Built with: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, MediaPipe hand tracking.
